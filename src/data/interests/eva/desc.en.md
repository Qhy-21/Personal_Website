# Neon Genesis Evangelion

*EVA*

Hideaki Anno uses battles between giant robots and Angels as an outer shell for a story about loneliness, existence, barriers to communication, and self-identity.

The AT Field is both a physical shield and the invisible "wall of the heart" between people.

Every time I watch it, I see myself in different characters: Shinji's avoidance, Asuka's pride, Rei's uncertainty about her identity, and Misato's contradictions and tenderness.
