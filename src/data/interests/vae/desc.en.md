# Vae

My favorite musician.

From *有何不可* to *乌鸦* and *出雨林记*, Vae's music has been part of growing up for generations of listeners.

With his unique voice and literary lyrics, Xu Song carved out his own territory in the Chinese music scene.

His songs echo with classical poetry, the loneliness of young people in the city, and a cool, detached view of the times.
